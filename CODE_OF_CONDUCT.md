# Code of Conduct

Tacqor is a place to build a programming language together. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space — is expected to keep it a place where people want to work.

## Expectations

- Be direct about the work and considerate of the person. Criticize code, designs and ideas; never people.
- Assume competence and good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Make room for newcomers: answer questions patiently, point to the document that explains something, and remember that the obvious was not obvious once.
- Keep disagreements technical. When a decision is contested, state the trade-off and let the maintainers decide; arguing past that point helps nobody.
- Respect people's time: write clearly, search before asking, and do not reopen settled discussions without new information.

## Not acceptable

Harassment of any kind; insults, demeaning comments or personal attacks; discriminatory language or conduct; publishing someone's private information; sexualized language or imagery; sustained disruption of discussions; and retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for the private channel, which is also used for conduct reports). Reports are read by the maintainers only, handled as quickly as possible, and kept confidential to the extent the situation allows. The person reported will not be told who reported them.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this document, warn the person responsible, decline contributions, or, for serious or repeated violations, ban the person from the project's spaces. Maintainers are held to this document like everyone else; a concern about a maintainer goes to another maintainer.

This document is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and by the codes of conduct of other language communities, written in the project's own words.
